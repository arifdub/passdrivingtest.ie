How it works for learners

The order the Irish system actually runs in, with the app keeping track of where you are.

1

Pass the theory test

Practise the questions and learn every sign. Free, no account needed to start.

2

Get your learner permit

Apply to the NDLS once your theory certificate is in hand.

3

Do your EDT

Twelve essential lessons with an approved driving instructor.

4

Pass your test

Mock tests, pre-test lessons, and a car for the day if you need one.

New here? The theory test is the first step, and it's free to practise — start with the questions, or read the full guide to the Driver Theory Test first: format, fees, booking and what happens on the day.

How it works for instructors

1
