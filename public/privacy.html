Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

PassDrivingTest.ie is a theory-test study tool and a marketplace connecting learner drivers in Ireland with ADI-registered driving instructors. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, who else ever sees it, and how to have it deleted.

1. Who runs this

PassDrivingTest.ie is operated from Ireland. For any question about this policy or your data, see Contact below. We are the controller of the personal data described here under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Irish data protection law.

2. What we collect

What we hold depends on which side of the platform you use.

Every account

  • Email address and password (the password itself is never stored in readable form — authentication is handled by our database provider, Supabase, using standard secure hashing).
  • Which kind of account it is — learner or instructor.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Your device's push-notification registration, if you turn notifications on — an identifier for your browser or phone, not your location or any content.
  • A light/dark display preference, kept only in your own browser (see Cookies & local storage).

If you are a learner

  • Your name, and a phone number if you give one.
  • Theory-test study progress — which topics and questions you have answered, and your mock-test results. If you are studying as a guest without an account, this stays on your own device and never reaches us.
  • When you request a lesson: the instructor, the time, a pick-up address if you give one, and any note you add.
  • Messages you send an instructor through the app, and any review you leave.

If you are an instructor

  • Your name, phone number, business name and a short bio, if you add them.
  • Your ADI registration number and category, and years of experience — checked by us, by hand, against the RSA's published register of approved driving instructors before your profile is made visible to learners.
  • What you teach, where you teach (counties, routing-key areas, test centres you cover), and your hourly rate.
  • The lessons, students and bookings you manage through the app, and the messages and reviews that come with them.

3. Why we collect it

  • To run the booking marketplace — matching learners with instructors, holding a requested lesson until it is accepted or declined, and keeping both sides' calendars straight.
  • To verify instructors — an ADI number is checked before a profile can be found by learners, so the badge means something.
  • To notify you — a booking request, a message, or a decision on your request is sent to you by email and, if you have turned it on, by push notification to your device.
  • To run the theory-test tool — tracking which questions you have seen and how you did, so your progress is where you left it.
  • To keep the platform working and safe — preventing abuse, debugging failures, and complying with the law.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it. This site runs no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Payment

This platform does not take any payment, and does not store any card or bank detail. Learners pay their instructor directly, by whatever means the two of you agree. If that ever changes, you will be told in advance, in the app, before it applies to anything you have already booked.

6. Where your data lives

Your data is held in a Supabase project — a managed Postgres database, with row-level security rules that decide, at the database itself, who may read or write each row. An instructor cannot read another instructor's students; a learner cannot read another learner's bookings.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, plus what we are required to keep for legal or accounting reasons after that.

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Account in the app. It takes immediate effect.

Deleting your account also deletes bookings shared with someone else — not just your side of them. A booking record belongs to both the learner and the instructor it was between. If you delete your account, every booking, message and review tied to it is removed completely, including the other person's copy of it: an instructor's record of a lesson that happened, or a learner's record of one they booked. This is not a setting we can turn off per request — it is how shared records work on this platform today. If that matters to you, say so when you contact us before deleting, and we will do what we reasonably can by hand.

Deleting your account removes your profile, login, photo, messages, bookings, reviews, availability settings and registered devices. It cannot be undone.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it, if it is wrong — or simply edit your own profile in the app.
  • Erase it — use Delete my account in the app, or contact us.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

To use any of these, contact us (below). You also have the right to complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission at any time.

9. Cookies & local storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's own local storage — which never leaves your device — to remember small things: your light/dark preference, and, if you study as a guest, your theory-test progress. Signing in moves that progress onto your account.

10. Age

This platform is built for people old enough to be studying for or holding an Irish driving licence. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from, children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will deal with it.
